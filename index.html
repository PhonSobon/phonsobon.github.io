I'm an AI/NLP engineer working within the Digital Transformation Working Group at Cambodia's Ministry of Post and Telecommunications, where I build Khmer‑language AI tools integrated into the Chaktomuk Management (CTM) system — the government's document intelligence platform.

My work centers on making Khmer, a low‑resource, unsegmented script, legible to machines: OCR for scanned government documents, image captioning, tag recommendation, text summarization, autocomplete, and speech‑to‑text. I publish models and datasets publicly on Hugging Face under phonsobon, and I'm currently pursuing a master's degree alongside this work.

If you're working on Southeast Asian or low‑resource NLP, or building for government digital transformation, I'd love to connect.
