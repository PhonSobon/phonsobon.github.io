Hi! I'm Phon Sobon, an AI Engineer with the Digital Transformation Working Group at the Ministry of Post and Telecommunications (MPTC) and a researcher specializing in Computer Vision and Natural Language Processing (NLP). My research interests include artificial intelligence, multimodal machine learning, computer vision, NLP, and large language models (LLMs). My goal is to develop AI solutions that address real-world challenges, particularly for khmer language.

I completed the Artificial Intelligence Professional Certificate at the American University of Phnom Penh (AUPP), where I was fortunate to be advised by Dr. Rina Buoy. I received my Bachelor's degree in Computer Science from the Royal University of Phnom Penh (RUPP). In addition, I completed the IT Expert Program, specializing in Full-Stack Development and Data Analytics, at the Institute of Science and Technology Advanced Development (ISTAD).
